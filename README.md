# Korvus

> AI Governance for Autonomous Agents

Korvus is an open AI governance reference architecture demonstrating how organizations can discover, inventory, assess, govern, and monitor AI systems and autonomous agents throughout their lifecycle.

---

## Vision

As AI agents take on increasingly autonomous business functions, governance must evolve beyond static policies.

Korvus explores what an enterprise-grade AI governance program should look like by combining:

- AI Governance
- Identity & Access Management
- Risk Management
- Agent Lifecycle Management
- Compliance Automation
