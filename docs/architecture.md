# Korvus Architecture

## Vision

Korvus is an AI governance platform for discovering, inventorying, governing, and monitoring enterprise AI systems and autonomous agents.

Rather than governing only AI models, Korvus focuses on the entire operational lifecycle of AI agents.

---

---

## Core Components

### AI Inventory

Maintains a centralized inventory of all enterprise AI systems and agents.

### Risk Engine

Assigns risk tiers using governance policies and organizational controls.

### Agent Registry

Maintains identities, ownership, permissions, and lifecycle information for autonomous agents.

### Governance Controls

Maps AI risks to organizational controls and evidence requirements.

### Monitoring

Tracks AI usage, drift, ownership changes, and compliance posture.
